## Who We Are

TechCheck Solutions is run by Adam — a tech enthusiast and IT professional based in Whitby, Ontario with years of hands-on experience in PC repair, networking, and web development.

After working in enterprise IT and running his own repair business, Adam started TechCheck Solutions to bring practical, no-nonsense tech help to everyday people and small businesses in the Durham Region and beyond.

## What We Do

This site is your go-to resource for:

- **PC tips and upgrade guides** — get more out of your machine without wasting money
- **Home server and Plex setup guides** — step-by-step for beginners
- **Honest buying guides** — cut through the marketing noise
- **Troubleshooting help** — common problems, simple solutions

We also offer hands-on services including PC repair, tune-ups, and web design for small businesses.

## Why TechCheck?

There's a lot of confusing, jargon-heavy tech content out there. Our goal is simple — make tech accessible, practical, and actually useful for real people. No fluff, no upsells, just honest advice.
