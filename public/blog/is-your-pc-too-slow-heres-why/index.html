Is Your PC Too Slow? Here's Why (And How to Fix It)

· 2 min read

Is your PC crawling when it used to fly? Before you rush out to buy a new one, there’s a good chance the fix is simpler — and cheaper — than you think.

Here are the most common reasons PCs slow down and exactly what to do about each one.

1. Too Many Startup Programs

Every time your PC boots up, it loads a bunch of programs in the background — many of which you never use. These eat up RAM and CPU before you even open a browser.

Fix: Press Ctrl + Shift + Esc to open Task Manager, click the Startup tab, and disable anything you don’t need running at boot.

2. Your Storage Drive Is Almost Full

When your hard drive or SSD gets close to full, your PC slows down significantly. Windows needs free space to operate properly.

Fix: Aim to keep at least 15–20% of your drive free. Delete files you don’t need, empty the recycle bin, and use Windows’ built-in Disk Cleanup tool.

3. You Haven’t Restarted in Days

It sounds basic but it works. Restarting clears your RAM, applies updates, and gives Windows a fresh start.

Fix: Restart your PC fully — not just sleep or hibernate — at least every couple of days.

4. Malware Running in the Background

Viruses and malware are notorious for silently consuming your PC’s resources.

Fix: Run a free scan with Malwarebytes and remove anything it finds.

5. Your PC Needs a Hardware Upgrade

If you’ve tried everything above and your PC is still slow, it might genuinely be underpowered for what you’re asking it to do. The two biggest upgrades that make an immediate difference are:

  • Adding more RAM — 8GB is the minimum in 2026, 16GB is ideal
  • Switching to an SSD — if you’re still on a hard drive, this is the single biggest speed boost you can make
